06 · Voice

Specific, calm, qualified.

Write with confidence about the method and humility about the conclusion. Never turn a probability into a verdict.

Do say

“At the selected threshold, the model flags this image for further review.”

Names the operating point and the human next step.

Avoid

“Our AI proves this image is fake.”

Overstates certainty and hides the role of calibration, context, and error.

07 · Data visualization
